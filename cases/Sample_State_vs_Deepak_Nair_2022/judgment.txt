TRAFFIC COURT, CHENNAI. Criminal Case No. 902 of 2022. State vs Deepak Nair (Accused). Judgment dated 14 February 2023.
FACTS: On 9 July 2022 at 8:40 pm, the accused drove his car at high speed on Anna Salai and hit a motorcycle, causing grievous injuries to the rider, Karthik. The accused was charged with rash and negligent driving.
ISSUES: Whether the accused drove rashly and negligently.
REASONING: Two eyewitnesses and the police speed-gun record of 92 km/h in a 50 km/h zone proved rash driving. The accused claimed brake failure but his vehicle inspection report showed the brakes working.
FINAL ORDER: The accused is convicted, sentenced to one year imprisonment and fined Rs. 10,000. His driving licence is suspended for 12 months. He shall also pay Rs. 2,00,000 to the victim as compensation.

IN THE COURT OF THE SESSIONS JUDGE, MYSURU
Sessions Case No. 45 of 2021
State (Prosecution) vs Rajesh Sharma (Accused)
Date of judgment: 20 August 2022

FACTS: The accused, Rajesh Sharma, is charged with theft of goods worth Rs. 2,00,000 from the godown of M/s Patil Traders on the night of 5 June 2021. The complaint was filed by the owner, Mahesh Patil, on 6 June 2021.

ISSUES: Whether the prosecution proved beyond reasonable doubt that the accused committed the theft.

REASONING: The prosecution relied on CCTV footage and the testimony of the watchman, Gopal. The watchman stated he saw a man in a blue shirt leave at 11 pm. The CCTV footage shows the person leaving at 1 am. The court found this time difference unexplained. The identification of the accused rested only on the watchman, who admitted he did not see the face clearly.

FINAL ORDER: The accused is acquitted, giving him the benefit of doubt. The accused shall be released immediately if not required in any other case.

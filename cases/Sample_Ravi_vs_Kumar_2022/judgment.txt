DISTRICT CONSUMER COMMISSION, BENGALURU. Complaint No. 310 of 2022. Priya (Complainant) vs Sunrise Builders Pvt Ltd (Opposite Party). Judgment dated 18 April 2023.
FACTS: Priya booked a 2BHK flat on 5 May 2019 and paid Rs. 28,00,000 of the Rs. 40,00,000 price. The builder promised possession by 5 May 2021 but had not delivered by the date of the complaint.
ISSUES: Whether the delay amounts to deficiency in service and what relief is due.
REASONING: The builder admitted the delay but blamed a labour shortage, which the Commission held was not a valid excuse as no force majeure clause covered it.
FINAL ORDER: The builder shall refund Rs. 28,00,000 with 8 percent interest from each payment date, pay Rs. 1,00,000 for mental agony and Rs. 25,000 as costs, within 45 days.

SMALL CAUSES COURT, MUMBAI. Eviction Suit No. 205 of 2021. Ashok Jain (Landlord) vs Mohan Pillai (Tenant). Judgment dated 6 July 2022.
FACTS: Mohan Pillai has rented a shop from Ashok Jain since 2012 at Rs. 15,000 per month. The landlord says rent was unpaid from April 2020 to March 2021, totalling Rs. 1,80,000, and also seeks the premises for his son's business.
ISSUES: (1) Whether the tenant is in arrears. (2) Whether eviction is justified.
REASONING: The tenant proved by receipts that rent was paid for six of the twelve months. Arrears for six months, Rs. 90,000, remained. The need of the son was not proved with any evidence.
FINAL ORDER: The eviction prayer is rejected. The tenant shall pay Rs. 90,000 arrears within 60 days, and continue paying monthly rent by the 7th of each month. If he defaults, the landlord may apply for eviction again.
